        Syntax cheatsheet

period 2026-11-02 .. 2026-11-29   # or: period 2026-11 · period 2026-11-02 for 4 weeks

               [avoid fri,night] [prefer weekend] [history 12] [max 8]

weight fairness|avoid|prefer|spread <n>
